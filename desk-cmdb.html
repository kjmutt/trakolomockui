The asset link is nullable on purpose. A business service was never bought, so it has no asset record; a laptop has both. Forcing procurement and topology into one table makes one of those two cases a lie.
Why this is a separate schema from SAM
sam.assets answers what did we buy, what did it cost, when does it renew. cmdb.configuration_items answers what runs where, and what breaks if this breaks. They overlap on physical kit and nowhere else — a software licence is only ever an asset, a business service is only ever a CI. The two are joined by a nullable foreign key rather than merged, which is what lets a change record show both the renewal date and the blast radius without either table carrying columns that are empty for most of its rows.
