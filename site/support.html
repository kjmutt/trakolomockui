Trakolo Support

For anything about your Trakolo workspace itself — a bug, a billing question, "how do I…" — sign in to the Self-Service Portal at support.trakolo.com and log a ticket. We work these the same way we'd work yours: no separate helpdesk, no extra tool.

Go to support.trakolo.com →
Support access is granted automatically when your license is issued.

What's the difference between this and my own service desk?

Your Trakolo workspace (yourcompany.trakolo.com) is where your team logs and tracks its own IT tickets. support.trakolo.com is Trakolo's own workspace — for issues with Trakolo the product itself, worked by our team.

Do I need a separate login for support.trakolo.com?

No — it's the same kind of Self-Service Portal sign-in you already use, just pointed at Trakolo's own workspace instead of your company's.
